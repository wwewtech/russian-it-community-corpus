# Dataset Usage Terms (Academic & Research Use)

> This file covers the **curated datasets** (Parquet, JSONL, and LoRA adapters) derived
> from public discussions. The **source code** of this repository is licensed separately
> under the MIT License — see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

The curated datasets are provided strictly for academic research, educational evaluation,
and linguistic study pursuant to Article 1274 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation
and international Fair Use principles. All personal identifiers have been de-identified
in accordance with GDPR Recital 26 and applicable privacy laws.

## Notice and Takedown

If you identify any inadvertent personal identifier or content that should be excluded,
please open a takedown issue or submit a removal request. Requests are processed within
48 hours.
